avoid starting a variable name with these characters. So, when you create a variable name, avoid starting the name with these characters. You can use them as subsequent characters, but do not begin the name with these characters.

=== ppt/slides/slide9.xml ===
Naming Conventions “NOTE”
For example, if you do not follow the naming convention and name your variable as – 
usertemporaryaddress
 instead of 
User_Temporary_Address
, your program will work fine, but you, as a programmer, will find it difficult to understand it later.
As mentioned earlier, these are naming conventions. This means they are not the rules, and if you do not follow the naming convention, Java will NOT throw any error. Your program will work just fine. However, it will not be easy for other programmers to understand your code, and you, too, may find it difficult to understand your code later.

JAVA OPERATORS
The Java operators are something that you use with your data or variables to perform certain operations. For example, let’s say that you have two ‘int’ variables, and you want to find the total of those variables. You use a plus operator (+ sign) for that, or if you want to subtract those two numbers, you use a minus operator (- sign) in your program. These are known as operators.
